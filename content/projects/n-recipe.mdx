For those who noticed my [gith](/projects/gith) release, I have exams again....
So I obviously started building something *again* instead of studying. 

The result this time: n-recipe (Friends & Family are still not sure what to call it, me neither),
an simple android app, to save and view recipes offline.  
One can for example create, edit and view recipes, calculate ingredients for more or less persons, 
it obviously features a catppucin theme with chooseable accent colors, as well as some other themes.
