For those who noticed my [gith](/projects/gith) release, I have exams again....
So I obviously started building something *again* instead of studying. 

The result this time: n-recipe (Friends & Family are still not sure what to call it, me neither, but now it's too late, guess that name stays :)),
an simple android app, to save, view and share recipes.
Most of the app works offline, although some parts, like publishing recipes for others to use obviously need an internet connection.  
One can also calculate ingredients for more or less persons, save & edit recipes from other people online, 
and it obviously features a catppucin theme with chooseable accent colors, as well as some other themes.

There are coming new features all the time, as this is currently my main free-time-project, check out Google Play for the latest features.  
Although I don't have that much free time, so features & improvements come in waves & with time :)
