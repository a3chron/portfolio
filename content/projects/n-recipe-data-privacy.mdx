n-recipe does not use any of your data apart from the data needed for the functionality of the app.  
n-recipe does not provide your data to any thrid parties, the app is 100% offline, 
all recipes and settings are stored on your device.

The code is open-source, everyone can view all relevant parts of it on [github](https://github.com/a3chron/n-recipe).
