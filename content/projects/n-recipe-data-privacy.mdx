**Effective Date: 19.09.2025**

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how n-recipe ("App") handles your information.

1. No Personal Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

All data (such as recipes) is stored locally on your device. The app does not access, transmit, or store this data externally.

2. Internet Connection

The app connects to the internet only to check if a newer version of the app is available (if you go to the settings), 
and to fetch the latest state of premium features beeing developed (If you view the premium features list - A offline backup version can be viewed without internet connection, it might not be up to date). 
No personal or usage data is transmitted during this process.

3. Data You Share

The app allows users to export, import, and share recipe files. You are solely responsible for any content you choose to share with others.
