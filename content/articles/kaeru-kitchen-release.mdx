# Kaeru's Kitchen release

For those that remember [Gith](/projects/gith): I had exams again...

For those who don't, I seem to have developed a habit during exam season - 
instead of studying, I end up procrastinating so hard, 
I start building small life-improvements for myself that sometimes end up being 
actually useful to others too.

Well, it's been a few months since the exams now, 
and finally we can see the results.

This time I was desperately needing a good recipe book. 
I decided to go for an app, so I could easily share recipes with family and friends, 
and maybe easier recipe writing.  
But of course all existing options either did not have to option to create own recipes, 
only browse them online, or they had a design, 
that looked like it was developed before computers became a thing (or were full of ads - equally horrifying for me to look at).  
The one good looking and working app I found was too old and not available for my android version.  
So I created my own.

The Android app I developed is a simple offline recipe book, 
which let's you save your own recipes, 
and share them with friends & family, or everyone using the app on the web-hub.

You can also quickly search recipes by category, 
depending on whether you are planning to cook dinner, 
bake a sweet dessert, or choose based on the time you have available. 
Another filter option is custom tags, so if your best friend is allergic to pineapples, 
you can easily filter out unsuitable recipes.

After deciding for the right recipe, the app offers to calculate how much of your ingredients 
you'll need for more or fewer servings, 
and then guide you through every step of the recipe, 
with all ingredients needed for this step, 
as well as an optional timer, when baking muffins for 20 minutes.

And for all the customization fanatics out there (myself included), 
besides dark / light mode, Kaeru's Kitchen features three different themes to choose from: "Material You", 
"Catppuccin" with many different selectable accent colors, 
as well as a theme following the style of Nothing Technology Limited.

But don't listen to me, check it out for yourself on [google play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.a3chron.nrecipe&utm_source=portfolio).
